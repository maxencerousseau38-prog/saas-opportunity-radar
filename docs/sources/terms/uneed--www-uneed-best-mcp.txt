Open data: monthly launch statistics
How many products are submitted, declined and launched on Uneed every month. Free to reuse with credit.
One JSON document, one row per month, with the definitions and the license included in every answer. Also available to agents as the get_launch_stats tool.
curl "https://mcp.uneed.best/v1/stats/launches" Copy
Field Meaning Since
month Calendar month (YYYY-MM). 2024-02
launched Products launched on Uneed in the month and still published, by launch date. 2024-02
submitted New products submitted to the waiting line in the month. A product sent back to the line is not counted again. 2026-08
declined Submissions declined by moderation in the month, on the date of the decision. 2026-08
complete False for the current month, whose counts are still growing.
estimated True for the months before the submission ledger started (5 November 2026).
Good to know
Months are calendar months in America/Los_Angeles, the timezone of Uneed launch days. The current month answers complete: false .
A month's launches are not a subset of that month's submissions: most products launch weeks or months after they are submitted, so the gap between the two is mostly the waiting line.
Declines are counted on the day of the decision, so a cleanup of older submissions lands in the month it happens.
Months before 5 November 2026 answer estimated: true : submissions are reconstructed and may run slightly high, and declines for August and September 2026 are a lower bound.
Refreshed hourly.
